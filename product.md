# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

England football fans — checking fixtures before the tournament, sharing the page with mates via WhatsApp and social, using it as the quick-reference answer to "when's the England game?" Used on mobile first, often in a pub or living room context. The audience ranges from casual viewers to dedicated supporters.

## Product Purpose

A single-page fixture guide for England's 2026 FIFA World Cup group stage. Shows all three group matches with opponent, stadium, kick-off times in both local US time and UK BST, and which terrestrial UK channel shows each game. Exists to be the screenshot people send each other — authoritative, at-a-glance, shareable.

## Brand Personality

Official, proud, match-day. The Football Association colour scheme: deep navy, Three Lions gold, St George's red. Feels like a premium match programme translated into digital — not a sports stats dashboard, not a generic world cup app.

## Anti-references

- Generic sports-stats SaaS dashboards (blue gradients, Roboto, data tables)
- Cream/beige AI landing page aesthetic
- Tourist-branding England (union jacks everywhere, red-white-blue bunting)
- American-sports aesthetic (sharp angles, neon, excessive motion)

## Design Principles

1. Clarity at a glance — every piece of information legible in under two seconds, even in a dark pub
2. Badge authority — the Three Lions crest leads; this is official England, not a fan site
3. Match-day atmosphere — dark, bold, proud; the page should feel like walking into a stadium
4. Screenshot-ready — the layout works as a shareable image, not just a webpage

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All flag imagery has descriptive alt text. Times labelled with timezone abbreviations so no ambiguity for diaspora fans abroad.
